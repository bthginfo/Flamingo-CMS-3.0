## Direction contract
THESIS: A modern law firm seen through the actual situations it serves. Replace cream-serif overlapping title boxes and grids of generic reassurance with a monumental photographic opening, an expansive interactive field of expertise, open portrait compositions and useful authored briefings.

OWN-WORLD: Clean white and very light cool gray; deep near-black navy, a controlled blue link accent; Manrope display at roughly 100–132px desktop and 50–62px mobile, 600–700 weight, tracking no tighter than -.04em. Body Manrope 17–19px, 1.65. Photography owns entire planes, not thumbnails. Thin rules, open lists, 12–16px media corners only where intentional. Precise light/dark contrast, no beige editorial serif, neon, glow, gradient type, icon tiles or generic 3-card rhythm.

STORY: Home identifies the firm and offer, lets the visitor recognize their situation, meet real counsel and locate a useful next step. Practice pages investigate different actual questions. Firm page reveals place and identity. Service page accomplishes downloading. People page makes the seven professionals tangible. Repeat a contact link when necessary, not whole paragraphs, imagery and processes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White solid original-logo navigation, a full-width near-black panoramic photographic stage. Large readable two-line title in the lower-left foreground, a short factual firm/offer lead, a clear consultation action, and an expansive high-quality contextual photograph across the remaining plane. No beige card overlay or stock courtroom cliches. Photo begins immediately below header and occupies most of the opening. Home title proposed “Mehr Klarheit.\nFür das, was zählt.” with eyebrow “Rechtsanwälte & Fachanwälte · Ingolstadt”. Use a 100% reliable on-photo text region/gradient scrim; retain recognizable image. Mobile keeps both title and image visible without header overlap. At desktop height 900–1000px show beginning of next content chapter. Other main-page openings deliberately differ: white oversized type with a panoramic lower image for firm, open contact sheet for team, typographic expandable index for practice directory, concise resource title for forms, real large portrait for lawyer pages.

FORM: Apple-informed photographic product chapter, the brief-pinned direction; seed key 3cb1de89. The signature interaction is the expertise explorer: five generous titled rows, one expanded topic with an image, specific questions and useful link; keyboard and native fallback work. Photography reveal and mild scroll progress are enhancements, no scroll hijack, never hide essential text on first paint.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
